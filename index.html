About me

I'm a first-year PhD student in condensed matter physics at MIT, working on the synthesis and characterization of bulk moiré materials and incommensurate superlattices in layered crystals. In my undergraduate studies at Harvard, I researched the application of novel thermal transport techniques to low-dimensional quantum materials, including thin film oxides and van der Waals quasicrystals.

Outside the lab, I like to watch science fiction, fold origami, and explore Boston.
